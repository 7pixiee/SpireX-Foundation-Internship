    Ways to eat with us

    Pick what suits your day. Open a row for details.

    Sixty seats across the main hall, the courtyard and the chef's counter. Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead guarantees your table, especially on weekends.

    Order by phone or collect in person. Our pizzas travel in vented boxes so the crust stays crisp. We deliver within 6 km of Koregaon Park from 12 pm to 10 pm.

    The upstairs room seats up to 24 for birthdays, farewells and small family dinners. We plan a set menu with you and you get a dedicated server for the evening.

    We cater office lunches and house parties for 15 to 150 guests. Ask for a tasting at the restaurant before you decide. Please give us at least three days' notice.
