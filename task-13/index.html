About the college

Established 1971
NAAC accredited, grade A

Meridian College was founded by a group of Pune educators who wanted an affordable college where teaching came first. Today it is an autonomous institution with four departments and a campus of 22 acres.

Faculty here teach undergraduates directly and supervise postgraduate research. Our graduates work in technology, finance, education and public service, and many go on to higher study in India and abroad.

Campus life

The campus covers 22 acres. Students have the space to study, train and take part in college activities.

Other facilities: hostels for 600 students, cafeteria, health centre, 800-seat auditorium, college bus service and Wi-Fi across campus.

Admissions 2027–28

Applications are open until 30 April 2027. Admission is based on qualifying examination marks, with an entrance test for MBA and M.Sc. applicants.

Eligibility

  • Undergraduate: Class 12 with at least 50% marks.
  • Postgraduate: a relevant bachelor's degree with at least 55% marks.
  • Reserved category applicants receive relaxation as per government norms.
